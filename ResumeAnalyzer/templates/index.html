How the Analysis Works

1

Parse

spaCy + NLTK extract your name, contact info, skills, education, experience, and certifications from your document.

2

Extract

A curated skill database cross-references both your resume and the JD, normalizing synonyms and aliases.

3

Match

TF-IDF vectorization and cosine similarity quantify keyword overlap, while rule-based logic scores education and experience.

4

Score

A weighted scoring model (Skills 50%, Experience 20%, Education 15%, Certs 10%, Keywords 5%) produces your final match score.
